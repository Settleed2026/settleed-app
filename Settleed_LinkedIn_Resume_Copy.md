For my EMBA capstone, I designed, built, and launched Settleed — a two-sided digital marketplace that connects Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) tenants with verified Section 8-ready landlords across metro Atlanta.

Drawing directly on my professional experience administering Georgia's HCV program, I translated deep knowledge of HUD regulations, RFTA workflows, and Housing Quality Standards (HQS) inspections into a full product requirements lifecycle — from 170+ customer discovery interviews through functional specifications, system design, third-party API integration, and end-to-end UAT.

**Key systems analyst work on this project:**
- Conducted 170+ structured discovery interviews with HCV tenants, independent landlords, housing counselors, and agency case managers to define and validate product requirements
- Authored user stories, functional specifications, and workflow diagrams for a two-sided platform with distinct permission sets, notification logic, and compliance rules for each user type
- Designed and executed a full UAT test plan across 12 functional areas — auth flows, landlord workflows, tenant workflows, payments, email notifications, and accessibility — logging and resolving 30+ defects prior to launch
- Integrated and documented API workflows across Stripe (payments), SendGrid (transactional email), Supabase (database and auth), and Cloudinary (document storage)
- Built a Georgia-wide HCV payment standard data model covering all 159 counties, translating HUD Fair Market Rent data into system business rules
- Applied Business Model Canvas methodology across 170+ interviews to validate problem/solution fit for both sides of the marketplace

*Settleed is live at settleed.com and was developed independently as an academic capstone project.*
