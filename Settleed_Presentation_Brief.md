### SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM (Part 1: Tenant Side)
**Layout:** Dark background. Large stat on left, supporting visual or icon on right.

**Headline:** *"The voucher has a clock. The system doesn't."*

**Key points (visual, not bullets):**
- HCV vouchers expire in 60–120 days
- Atlanta has one of the tightest rental markets in the Southeast
- Tenants describe spending weeks manually calling landlords, only to be told "we don't accept Section 8"
- The RFTA (Request for Tenancy Approval) process is paper-based, routed manually through housing authorities — adding 2–6 weeks of delay after a tenant finds a unit
- **Quote from interviews:** *"By the time I found a place that took vouchers, my voucher was almost gone."* (paraphrase from discovery)

**Visual suggestion:** Timeline graphic showing voucher expiry window vs. average days-to-lease

---

### SLIDE 3 — THE PROBLEM (Part 2: Landlord Side)
**Layout:** Two-column. Left: barrier icons in circles. Right: key stat or landlord quote.

**Headline:** *"Landlords don't hate Section 8. They hate the paperwork."*

**Key points:**
- Most "we don't accept Section 8" decisions come from fear of inspection failure, RFTA delays, and uncertainty about when HUD pays
- Independent (mom-and-pop) landlords have no staff to manage the administrative overhead
- The inspection process (HQS) has no digital tracking — landlords wait with no visibility
- **Evidence from interviews:** Of landlords interviewed who didn't accept Section 8, majority cited administrative friction as the #1 reason — not the tenant population

**Visual suggestion:** Icon row — 4 barriers landlords named (Paperwork / Inspection uncertainty / RFTA timeline / Late payment fear)

**Landlord customer:**
- Independent ("mom-and-pop") landlords, 1–4 residential units, Atlanta/DeKalb/Fulton
- Why chosen: Largest underrepresented landlord segment; lowest administrative capacity; highest sensitivity to friction reduction
- **Evidence:** [X] landlord interviews; majority open to Section 8 if process were simpler; confirmed willingness to pay for tools that reduce their back-and-forth with housing authorities

### SLIDE 5 — THE VALUE WE BRING (Tenants)
**Layout:** 2x2 icon grid or left-column icons with right-side headline.

**Headline:** *"Find it faster. Track it yourself. Move in."*

**Four value points (each with icon + short label — no bullets):**
1. **Pre-screened landlord listings** — Only landlords who accept vouchers. Zero cold rejections.
2. **Voucher Math Calculator** — Instantly see if a unit's rent is within your payment standard before you call.
3. **RFTA Status Tracker** — Know where your paperwork is at every step, without calling the housing authority.
4. **Digital application** — Apply once, reuse across listings. No paper, no fax.

**Evidence anchor:** *"Validated across [X] tenant interviews — RFTA visibility and pre-screened listings were the top two requested features."*

### SLIDE 6 — THE VALUE WE BRING (Landlords)
**Layout:** Same icon grid style as Slide 5, mirrored for visual consistency.

**Headline:** *"Less paperwork. Faster inspections. Qualified tenants."*

**Four value points:**
1. **Expedited RFTA portal** — Submit and track RFTA digitally. No more faxes, no more waiting blind.
2. **Pre-inspection checklist** — Know exactly what HQS inspectors look for before they arrive.
3. **Tenant pre-screening badge** — Every applicant has a verified active voucher. No unqualified inquiries.
4. **GA Rent Analyzer** — Free tool showing the Section 8 payment standard for any ZIP code — so landlords know their rent qualifies before they list.

**Evidence anchor:** *"Validated across [X] landlord interviews — pre-screening and RFTA visibility were the two highest-value features cited."*
